**Effective date:** 2026-08-03

This Acceptable Use Policy (the **Policy**) applies to every installation, deployment, copy, modification, integration, and use of Munin.

Munin is a general-purpose cybersecurity and threat-intelligence platform. Access to the software, its source code, its tools, or its outputs does not grant authorization to access, test, scan, monitor, collect data from, interfere with, or otherwise interact with any system, account, network, service, person, or organization.

## Authorized use only

You may use Munin only when all of the following are true:

- you have the legal right and all necessary prior authorization to perform the activity;
- the targets, techniques, data sources, duration, and operational boundaries are clearly defined;
- your use complies with the LICENSE, this Policy, and all applicable laws, regulations, contracts, and third-party terms;
- you maintain appropriate human oversight for actions that may affect systems, data, accounts, or third parties; and
- you stop the activity when authorization expires, scope becomes unclear, or unexpected impact occurs.

A successful login, deployment, tool call, model response, generated command, approval prompt, or technical capability does not establish authorization.

## Prohibited use

You may not use Munin to:

- access or attempt to access systems, accounts, networks, services, or data without explicit authorization;
- exceed an authorized scope, evade a restriction imposed by a system owner, or continue after authorization has been withdrawn;
- deploy malware, ransomware, destructive payloads, credential theft, persistence, evasion, phishing, fraud, extortion, or unauthorized surveillance;
- damage, disrupt, degrade, overload, manipulate, or interfere with systems, services, data, or users;
- obtain, retain, disclose, sell, transfer, or exploit credentials, personal data, confidential information, or other data without a lawful basis and authorization;
- conceal unlawful activity, falsify evidence, impersonate another party, or misrepresent the source or authorization of an operation;
- facilitate violations by another person or provide Munin as a service for prohibited activity; or
- use Munin in any manner that creates an unreasonable risk of harm to people, property, systems, organizations, or the public.
