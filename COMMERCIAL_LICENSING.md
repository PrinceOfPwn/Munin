# Munin Commercial Licensing

Munin is source-available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). The community license does **not** authorize commercial use.

This document explains when a separate commercial license is required. It is not itself a commercial license and does not replace or amend the LICENSE.

## When a commercial license is required

You must obtain a separate written commercial license before using Munin for any direct or indirect commercial purpose or benefit, including:

- use by or for a for-profit business, company, consultancy, contractor, sole proprietor, or commercial team;
- internal use that supports business operations, reduces costs, improves productivity, manages risk, or creates a competitive advantage;
- paid security research, threat intelligence, incident response, red-team, penetration-testing, audit, assessment, training, or consulting work;
- managed services, hosted deployments, software-as-a-service, platform access, or customer-facing use;
- resale, relicensing, paid distribution, paid support, implementation, customization, or integration;
- incorporation into a commercial product, workflow, report, dataset, model, service, or deliverable;
- use in work performed for compensation, even when Munin is not separately itemized or charged to the customer;
- evaluation, prototyping, or research undertaken with an anticipated commercial application; or
- any other use intended to generate revenue, compensation, cost savings, fundraising value, market advantage, or another financial benefit.

The absence of revenue from a specific execution does not automatically make the use noncommercial when it supports a commercial activity or anticipated commercial application.

## Commercial license scope

A commercial license may define:

- the licensed organization and affiliates;
- permitted users, deployments, environments, and use cases;
- internal use, customer work, managed services, redistribution, or embedding rights;
- term, fees, reporting, support, updates, and audit obligations;
- branding, attribution, confidentiality, and security requirements; and
- warranty, liability, indemnity, and termination terms.

No commercial rights are granted unless they are contained in a separate written agreement authorized by the copyright holder. Purchase orders, informal conversations, issue comments, pull requests, contributions, or use of the public repository do not create commercial permission.

## Requesting a commercial license

Contact the repository owner through a private contact method listed on the GitHub profile or another verified project channel. Include:

- the organization or individual requesting the license;
- the intended use case;
- whether the use is internal, customer-facing, hosted, redistributed, or embedded;
- the expected number of users and deployments; and
- the requested term and support requirements.

Do not place confidential business, customer, target, credential, or security information in a public GitHub issue.

## Open-source and community contributions

A contribution to Munin does not automatically grant the contributor or their employer commercial-use rights. Contributions remain subject to the repository's contribution terms and the applicable project license.

## Questions about classification

When there is uncertainty about whether a planned use is commercial, treat it as requiring a commercial license until written clarification is obtained from the copyright holder.
